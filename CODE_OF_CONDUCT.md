# Code Of Conduct

Orchestra's community should be useful, direct, and welcoming.

## Expected Behavior

- Treat other participants with respect.
- Discuss ideas and code on their merits.
- Give actionable feedback and assume ordinary mistakes are made in good faith.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.
- Make room for people with different backgrounds and levels of familiarity with the project.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, sustained personal attacks, and publishing private information are not accepted. Spam, trolling, and disruption of project spaces are also not accepted.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participants whose behavior harms the community. Enforcement decisions consider severity, repetition, context, and the safety of affected people.

Report conduct concerns privately to a maintainer using the contact information on the Platform-Studio GitHub organization. Do not open a public issue about a private conduct report. Maintainers will limit information to the people needed to review and respond to the report.

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces where someone is representing the Orchestra project.
