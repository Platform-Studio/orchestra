# Judging Xmas Movies
You must judge whether a movie should reasonably be considered a Christmas movie, rather than merely a movie that happens to contain Christmas scenes.

Do not rely primarily on how the studio markets it, its conventional genre classification, or whether audiences traditionally watch it at Christmas. Instead, assess how integral Christmas is to the movie itself.

## Criteria
Consider several dimensions:

- Plot dependence: would the story require substantial rewriting if moved to another time of year?
- Does Christmas explain why characters are together, create the circumstances for the plot, or cause important events?
- Character motivation and themes: are characters' goals, relationships, reconciliation, family, generosity, loneliness, homecoming, etc. meaningfully connected to Christmas?
- Setting and presentation: how extensively does the movie use Christmas music, decorations, traditions, dialogue, imagery and atmosphere? Distinguish pervasive and deliberate use from a few incidental Christmas references.

## Use a Counterfactual Test
Apply a counterfactual test: imagine replacing Christmas with an ordinary week in July. If only superficial details need changing - remove some decorations, change a party, replace a song - Christmas is probably just a setting.

If character motivations, major plot mechanics, important scenes, or thematic meaning stop making sense, that is strong evidence that it is genuinely a Christmas movie. Conversely, a movie does not need to be about Christmas, feature Santa, or belong to the family/comedy genre to qualify. An action, horror, romance, or thriller can still be a Christmas movie if Christmas is sufficiently integral to its identity.

## Make a Holistic Judgment
Make a holistic judgment rather than applying a rigid checklist. Classify the movie as Yes, Borderline, or No, and briefly explain the strongest evidence on both sides before giving the verdict. Give particular weight to whether Christmas is structural rather than merely decorative: the best test is not simply "Does Christmas appear in this movie?" but "Would this meaningfully be a different movie without Christmas?"
